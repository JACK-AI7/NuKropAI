# NuKropAI: Empowering Farmers with AI Intelligence

NuKropAI is a cutting-edge agriculture intelligence platform designed to help farmers maximize yields, identify diseases instantly, and stay ahead of market trends. Built for precision agriculture, NuKropAI puts the power of a professional agronomist in your pocket.

## Key Features:

### 🔍 Instant Disease Diagnosis
Scan your crops with our advanced AI vision model to identify over 40+ common pests and diseases in seconds. Get immediate, actionable advice on treatment and prevention.

### 🛰️ Satellite Intelligence (NDVI)
Monitor your farm's health from space. Our NDVI integration provides real-time vegetation health maps, helping you identify underperforming zones before they impact your yield.

### ⛈️ Hyper-Local Weather Insights
Stay prepared with high-precision weather forecasting tailored to your exact coordinates. Receive alerts for rain, heatwaves, and potential disease-favorable conditions.

### 📊 Real-Time Mandi Prices
Get the best value for your harvest. Monitor live Mandi prices across regional markets and use our AI price predictor to choose the best time to sell.

### 💬 AI Farming Advisor
Have a question about irrigation, fertilizers, or crop rotation? Chat with our AI advisor trained on the latest agricultural research and local farming conditions.

### 📍 Multi-Farm Management
Register and manage multiple fields with ease. Track health history, soil reports, and scan records for each farm separately.
