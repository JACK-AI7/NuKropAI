# Privacy Policy for NuKropAI

**Last Updated: May 12, 2026**

NuKropAI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our mobile application and related services.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Location Data
We collect precise and approximate location data to provide hyper-local weather forecasts, Mandi prices, and satellite intelligence for your specific farm coordinates.

### Camera and Media
We access your camera and photo library only when you initiate a "Crop Scan" or upload an image for analysis. These images are processed to identify pests and diseases.

### Farm Information
Data about your farm (name, crop types, area) is collected to personalize your farming advice and yield predictions.

### Usage Data
We collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports via Google Firebase to improve app stability and performance.

## 2. How We Use Information
* To provide and maintain our services.
* To provide localized agricultural intelligence.
* To improve our AI models for disease detection.
* To monitor app performance and fix crashes.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with service providers (like Google Firebase) necessary for app operation.

## 4. Your Rights
You can edit your profile information or delete your account at any time within the app settings.

## 5. Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

## 6. Contact Us
For questions about this policy, please contact us at support@nukrop.ai
