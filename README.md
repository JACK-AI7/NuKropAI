# 🌾 NuKropAI - The Ultimate AI Operating System for Agriculture

NuKropAI is an advanced, fully-functional, real-time Agriculture OS built specifically for farmers. It leverages the latest AI technology, live government APIs, and hyperlocal weather data to provide an unmatched, 100% functional full-stack solution directly on mobile devices.

Designed and Developed by **B. JASWANTH REDDY**.

---

## 🚀 Core Features (100% Real-World Functional)

- **AI Crop Doctor & Disease Scanner**: Uses Gemini Vision AI to analyze photos of crops/soil and instantly provides diagnosis, remedies, and treatment plans in your local language (Voice Text-to-Speech included).
- **Live Mandi Prices & Autopilot**: Connects directly to government `api.data.gov.in` to fetch the absolute latest Mandi prices based on your GPS location. Includes AI trend analysis and price crash predictions.
- **Background Market & Weather Alerts**: Utilizes Android WorkManager to monitor target prices in the background. If a price hike or rain is detected, a high-priority system notification is instantly sent.
- **Hyperlocal Open-Meteo Weather**: No mock data. Gives you exact temperature, rain, wind, and humidity forecasts based on your exact latitude and longitude.
- **AI Subsidy & Scheme Matcher**: Uses LLM intelligence to match your farm profile (State, Size, Crop) to the latest 2024 active PM-KISAN, PMFBY, and state-specific agricultural schemes.
- **Cloud & Local Memory**: Powered by Supabase for authentication and Room Database for seamless, offline-first local memory saving.
- **True Multilingual Support**: Change the language on the fly. The entire OS instantly translates to Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, and English.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose (100% Declarative UI)
- **Backend / Auth**: Supabase Auth (Magic Links, OAuth)
- **Local Database**: Android Room DB (SQLite)
- **AI Vision / Intelligence**: OpenRouter / Google Gemini Vision APIs
- **Background Processing**: Android WorkManager & Coroutines
- **Real-Time Data**: Open-Meteo API, Indian Government Open Data (Agmarknet)
